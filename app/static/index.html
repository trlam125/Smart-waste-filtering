ACCURATE SCANNING TIPS

Improve AI recognition accuracy

01

Good lighting

Avoid dark scenes or strong backlighting so the AI can capture colors and surface textures accurately.

02

Scan multiple objects

You can place multiple waste items in the same frame. Keep the objects reasonably separated so the detector can locate them more accurately.

03

Close-up framing

Move close enough for the waste item to fill about 60-80% of the frame. The AI handles portrait and landscape orientation automatically, but keep the entire object near the center when possible.
